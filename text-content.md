# KIDSLINK

Your Health Comes First

Kidslink Children’s Clinic provides specialized medical services to babies and children in Singapore. We have 4 clinics located in Bedok, Choa Chu Kang, Jurong and Sengkang.

Our Pediatricians are well trained in general pediatrics and many sub-specialties including cardiology, respiratory medicine, infectious diseases and gastroenterology.

We provide baby and children immunization, developmental assessment and general pediatric consultation.
